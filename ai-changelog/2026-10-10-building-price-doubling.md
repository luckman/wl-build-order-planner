# Building price doubling

Corrected Production and Racial building prices to base cost × 2^(copy number − 1). Barracks now cost 200/150, 400/300, 800/600, and 1600/1200 for the first four copies. Earlier planned copies continue to reserve their place before workers arrive; upgrades and Economic/Defensive buildings retain fixed prices.

Updated pricing, simulation, and action-card regression expectations and documented the corrected formula in the README.
