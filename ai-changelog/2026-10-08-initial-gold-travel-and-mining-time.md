# Initial gold travel and mining time — 2026-10-08

- Changed the default gold mining time from 3.0 to 3.10 seconds.
- Initial gold miners now take one second less on their first trip from the
  Castle/Citadel to the mine.
- All later outbound gold trips, all gold return trips, and trips by newly
  trained workers continue to use the configurable 4.0-second travel defaults.
- Added simulator coverage for the opening-trip exception and the normal
  follow-up trip.
