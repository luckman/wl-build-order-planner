# Faction-specific profile labels

- Player level inputs use Castle, Farm, Factory, and Sanctuary for Light, and Citadel, Pigsty, Workshop, and Dark Gate for Dark. Sawmill and Barracks labels stay the same.
- The derived food summary also uses Farm or Pigsty according to the selected faction.
- Switching factions or importing a faction updates labels while preserving the shared profile fields and level values. JSON names and simulation rules are unchanged.
