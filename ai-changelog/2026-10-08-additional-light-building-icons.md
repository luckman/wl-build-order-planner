# Additional Light building icons

- Prepared and registered transparent artwork for Factory, Sanctuary, Shipyard, Dwarven Forge, Human Monument, Elven Tree, Altar of Heroes, and Tower of Light Magic.
- Used the owner-confirmed screenshot mapping: top row Factory, Shipyard, Human Monument, Altar of Heroes; bottom row Sanctuary, Dwarven Forge, Elven Tree, Tower of Light Magic.
- Removed card frames, level badges, counters, upgrade arrows, backdrops, and ambient particles while retaining each building's base and intrinsic magical effects.
- These buildings need confirmed costs, build times, producers, and prerequisites before adding schedulable catalogue entries.
- Used the built-in image editing tool. Each background-extraction prompt specifies the screenshot card position and building description, asks to preserve the design, materials, colors, perspective, and game-art style, and isolates the complete building on a transparent square canvas without interface elements.
- Saved the eight assets as `src/icons/buildings/light-{factory,sanctuary,shipyard,dwarven-forge,human-monument,elven-tree,altar-of-heroes,tower-of-light-magic}.png`.
