# Building types

Added a `type` field to building records and town-hall upgrades in `src/game/data.json`. Allowed categories are `Economic`, `Production`, `Racial`, and `Defensive`.

- Light: Castle, Castle lvl 2, Sawmill, and Farm are Economic; Barracks is Production.
- Dark: Citadel, Citadel lvl 2, Sawmill, and Pigsty are Economic; Barracks is Production.

Unit records are unchanged.
