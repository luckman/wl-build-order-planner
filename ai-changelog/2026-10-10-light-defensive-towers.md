# Light defensive towers

Added Tower of Light Magic (250 gold / 350 wood, 30 seconds) requiring Elven Tree level 1, Crossbow Tower (150 / 300, 25 seconds) requiring Human Monument level 1, and Artillery Tower (200 / 400, 35 seconds) requiring Dwarven Forge level 1.

All three are Defensive buildings using existing Light icons, fixed per-copy prices, worker construction timing, and automatic prerequisite insertion. No unconfirmed limits were added. Updated documentation and game-rules revision to 2026-10-10.5.
