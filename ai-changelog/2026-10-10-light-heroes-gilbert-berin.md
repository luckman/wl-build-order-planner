# Light heroes Gilbert and Berin

Added Gilbert (500 gold / 500 wood / 8 food, 45 seconds) requiring Altar of Heroes level 1 and Human Monument level 1, and Berin (500 gold / 700 wood / 9 food, 45 seconds) requiring Altar of Heroes level 1 and Dwarven Forge level 1.

Both recruit through Altar of Heroes, use their existing icons, and inherit hero food reservations, production queues, and automatic dependency insertion. No unconfirmed maximum counts were added. Updated README and catalog documentation.
