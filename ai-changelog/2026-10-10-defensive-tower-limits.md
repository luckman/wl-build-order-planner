# Defensive tower limits

Added highest-level limits to all three Light Defensive buildings: Tower of Light Magic follows Elven Tree, Crossbow Tower follows Human Monument, and Artillery Tower follows Dwarven Forge. Each allows one tower at racial level 1, two at level 2, and three at level 3; absent prerequisites allow zero.

Existing capacity logic counts reserved constructions, uses the highest completed racial level rather than summing buildings, inserts upgrades when choosing additional towers, and rejects a fourth copy. Updated documentation and rules revision to 2026-10-10.6.
