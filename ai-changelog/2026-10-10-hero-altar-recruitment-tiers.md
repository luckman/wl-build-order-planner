# Hero recruitment tiers

Added a shared code rule requiring Altar level 1 for the first hero, level 2 for the second, and level 3 for the third, independent of hero type. Queued and recruited heroes count across all Altars, while failed orders do not. The recruiting Altar must meet the tier after upgrade completion and order delay.

Hero selection inserts missing Altar upgrades and their prerequisites. Removing heroes recalculates the tiers; per-type uniqueness and racial prerequisites remain enforced. Updated documentation and bumped game rules to 2026-10-10.2.
