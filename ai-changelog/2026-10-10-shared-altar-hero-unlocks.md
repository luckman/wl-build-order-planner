# Shared Altar hero unlocks

Corrected hero recruitment tiers to require at least one Altar at the required level across all Altars. Once unlocked, any available Altar can recruit the second or third hero, including a level 1 Altar. The upgraded Altar can be busy recruiting while another handles recruitment.

Preserved completion and order-delay gating, automatic upgrade insertion, queued-hero tier reservations, and per-type uniqueness. Updated documentation and game-rules revision to 2026-10-10.3. Added regression coverage for recruitment in lower-level Altars.
